the fine print

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, phone number, email and project details when you message us on WhatsApp, email us or fill in the brief builder. This site has no sign-up and does not ask for payment details.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare quotes and invoices, deliver your project and keep you updated. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Cookies and storage

The site remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser. Fonts and the 3D library load from Google Fonts and Cloudflare, which may log basic technical data such as your IP address.

Your photos, videos and files

Material shot or shared for your project is used only for your project. We show work in our portfolio only with your permission.

Third parties

Payments run through regulated providers such as Razorpay or your bank's UPI app, under their own privacy policies. Messages sent on WhatsApp are covered by WhatsApp's policy.

Your choices

Ask us anytime to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you: info@bixmedia.in or +91 62892 32792.
